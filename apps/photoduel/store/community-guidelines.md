# PhotoDuel community guidelines — DRAFT

**Purpose:** photos in PhotoDuel are shared only with people you invite. Keep them good for everyone in the room.

## Allowed
- Photographs you took or have the right to share, that fit the prompt.
- Playful, creative and imperfect photos.

## Not allowed
- Nudity or sexual content of any kind.
- Violence, gore, self-harm, or dangerous acts.
- Hate, harassment, threats or bullying toward anyone.
- Photos of people who have not agreed to be photographed and shared, including minors.
- Identifying information: addresses, licence plates, ID documents, screenshots of messages, or private documents.
- Images that infringe someone else's copyright or trademark.
- Spam, advertising, or content meant to manipulate the vote (for example, asking members to vote for a particular photo in a way that breaks the rules).
- Attempts to find or contact people outside the app.

## Voting honesty
- You vote once per challenge, for one photo that is not your own.
- Votes cannot be changed. Do not try to influence another member's vote.

## What happens when you report content
- Reports go to the operator's moderation team. The reported person is not told who reported them.
- If three different members report the same photo, it is hidden automatically until it is reviewed.
- Moderators may remove content, suspend an account, or dismiss a report. Serious or unlawful content is escalated.
- Target: review each report within 24 hours. This is an internal operating target, not a guarantee of response time.

## Blocking
Blocking hides the other person's photos from you, and you cannot vote for each other's photos.
