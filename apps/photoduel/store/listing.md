**Title:** PhotoDuel: Friend Photo Challenges
**Short description (80 characters max):** Private photo challenges with friends. Vote for the best shot.
**Full description:**
PhotoDuel turns your friend group into a private photo challenge.

1. Create a room and invite friends with a link.
2. Pick a prompt from our curated list, such as "Find a perfect circle" or "Capture an interesting shadow".
3. Everyone submits one photo before the deadline.
4. Everyone votes once for a photo that is not their own.
5. See the result when voting closes.

Rooms are private. Only the people you invite can see your photos. There are no public profiles, no feeds and no chat.
