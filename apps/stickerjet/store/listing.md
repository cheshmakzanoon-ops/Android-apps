## Title
StickerJet

## Short description (draft, under 80 characters)
Turn a short video into an animated sticker. Stays on your phone.

## Full description (draft)
StickerJet makes an animated sticker from a short video you pick from your gallery.

- Trim a loop of 0.5 to 3 seconds and preview it.
- Optionally remove the background from a person. Correct the edges with a brush.
- Add a caption, scale and move the picture.
- Export a 512-pixel animated WebP of up to 500 KB and share it as a file.

StickerJet has no account and no network access. Your video stays on your device until you choose to share.

Limits: background removal works on people only. Other subjects keep their background. StickerJet does not add stickers to any messaging app's sticker library; you share the file yourself.

## Claims this listing must not make
- That a messaging app installs the sticker as a pack.
- That background removal works on pets, objects or every person.
- That the app is tested on a range of devices. Only an emulator has run it so far.
- Any figure about quality, speed or battery.
